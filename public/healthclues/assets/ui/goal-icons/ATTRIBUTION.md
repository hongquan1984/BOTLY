# HealthClues goal-category icons

These twelve compact SVG line icons were drawn for HealthClues and are project-created artwork. They use no third-party icon paths, fonts, stock art, or embedded text, and are tinted by the existing CSS-mask game icon component.

The seven discovery mappings are exported as `HEALTHCLUES_DISCOVERY_ICONS` in `src/components/healthclues/HealthCluesGameIcon.tsx`. The five additional gameplay categories cover hydration, nutrition, movement, relaxation, and everyday habits. No AI goal title or target is encoded in an illustration.

The four separate My Journey icons remain adapted from Game-icons.net under CC BY 3.0; see `../my-journey/ATTRIBUTION.md` for creators, pinned source revision, and attribution.
