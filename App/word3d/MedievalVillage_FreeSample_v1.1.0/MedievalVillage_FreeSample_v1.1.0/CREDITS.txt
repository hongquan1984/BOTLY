Author / art direction: Sidiq Pratomo

About the art
- Every asset is original work by the author: low-poly 3D models built
  in Blender, a stylized texture atlas and a painted foliage sheet.
- No generative image or 3D model (text-to-image, text-to-3D, diffusion,
  etc.) was used, and no third-party models, textures, photos, HDRIs or
  scans were used as input.
- The shop signs, stained glass and other designs in the textures are
  original and do not reproduce existing artworks.
- Store screenshots and the images in Previews/ show only assets from
  this pack.
