SCALE AND PIVOTS
  Real-world metres, everything stands on the ground. Buildings, props and
  plants: pivot at the centre of the base, main front facing -Y in Blender.
  Ground tiles: 2 x 2 m, pivot at a corner, seamless in every direction.
  Kit walls: 2 m wide and 3 m tall, running along +X from the pivot, outside
  facing -Y. A 4 m deep house: walls round a 4 m wide footprint, a gable on
  each end wall, roof bays (EndL, Mid..., EndR) on the front slope and the
  same pieces turned 180 degrees for the back slope, Ridge on top.

MATERIALS
  M_Village_Atlas    T_Village_Atlas.png as base colour; T_Village_Emission.png
                     as emission for night scenes (strength 0 by day, 1.5-2 at
                     night) lights the windows.
  M_Village_Glow     lamp glass: the atlas as emission, strength about 1-3.
  M_Village_Foliage  leaves: T_Village_Foliage.png with its alpha as cutout
                     mask (0.5), one-sided.
  The tree and bush meshes carry a "Wind" vertex colour layer (R sway,
  G flutter, B phase) for wind shaders.

THE FULL PACK ADDS
