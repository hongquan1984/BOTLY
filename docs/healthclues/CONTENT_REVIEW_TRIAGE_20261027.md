## External source option for a real recipe candidate

This is a source-based candidate for a reviewer to consider, not a new static seed, database import, or approval:

- [USDA MyPlate: Hearty Salmon Skewers over Brown Rice](https://www.myplate.gov/recipes/hearty-salmon-skewers-over-brown-rice) presents a real recipe and attributes the recipe to Seafood Nutrition Partnership. The page lists preparation as 20 minutes and cooking as 15 minutes (35 minutes total, calculated by addition), and links the recipe steps. A concise HealthClues card could say: “Salmon, cherry tomato, and pineapple skewers served with brown rice. The source lists 20 minutes preparation and 15 minutes cooking.” Link out to MyPlate rather than copying its full recipe. Do not label it as weight-loss guidance or claim a clinical benefit.
- Safety review needed: the recipe contains fish, calls for skewers, and describes cooking salmon in a skillet. The [USDA FSIS safe temperature chart](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart) says fish should reach 145°F (62.8°C) measured with a food thermometer. An editor should determine how to present that safety note without implying MyPlate's method itself ensures it. The total 35-minute duration does not match a five-minute preference; only suggest it when the user's available-time choice permits it.
- This source page is hosted by USDA MyPlate but credits Seafood Nutrition Partnership as the recipe source. Confirm source suitability, link availability, localization and editorial rights before adding it to the catalog. Until then it remains a review suggestion only.
